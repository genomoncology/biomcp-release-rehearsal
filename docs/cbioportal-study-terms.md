# cBioPortal study terms

cBioPortal remains available in all hosted modes with its `attribution` source label. In `restricted`, BioMCP leaves out studies whose reviewed study information specifies noncommercial terms or an excluded purpose limit. This applies to study search, saved search continuations, every study section, and variant recurrence pages. Replies name the omitted studies and the reason. A left-out study does not make a reply partial: the status stays complete and the notice says what was left out. A direct read or recurrence call for a flagged study has no record in its result, and its cBioPortal source row is `skipped` with reason `disabled` and that notice. Read that row as a policy omission, not as a study with no data; a study missing from the catalog returns `absent`. `commercial`, `academic`, local CLI and stdio retain their existing study access. A caller key does not override a study flag in `restricted`.

The flags apply when reading results. Existing catalogs, cached source replies and stored search pools remain readable without synchronizing, changing the store format or changing cache identities. Filtering precedes paging, so study totals and continuation offsets describe the permitted rows. Recurrence for a flagged study returns no observations or counts.

## Reviewed public catalog

Read on 2026-11-06: [public detailed study list](https://www.cbioportal.org/api/studies?projection=DETAILED), 551 studies. The returned JSON had SHA-256 `1345b97d3f636b8ed86339abd55e8b63f5c2c9f38bdf4323b22400f3d1ee91f2`. The restriction wording lives in each study's `description` field, not its access groups or public-read flag.

The [checked-in flag list](../crates/biomcp-adapters/src/cbioportal/commercial-restrictions.tsv) records each flagged study ID, exclusion reason and exact restriction wording. Nine descriptions explicitly specify the Creative Commons BY-NC-ND 4.0 license. Eight link to [the license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/deed.en); the ninth names it in plain text. Ten additional studies carry purpose limits: five TARGET studies limit use to pediatric biomedical research and five studies limit use to research and bar clinical decision-making. The reviewed policy excludes all ten in `restricted`. This nineteen-study list is the shared source for all study filtering.

When the public study list changes, review the descriptions and refresh the flag list in the same change. Copy the exact study ID and restriction wording for explicit noncommercial wording or an owner-approved purpose limit. Check new, changed and removed studies. Keep studies without explicit noncommercial wording or an owner-approved purpose-limit flag enabled. Send ambiguous wording to the product owner for a decision; do not infer a commercial restriction from research-only language, access groups, public availability or a citation.

The purpose-limit exclusions implement the product owner’s explicit ruling. Local, `commercial` and `academic` study access remains available. This policy does not expand paper and article-text restrictions.
