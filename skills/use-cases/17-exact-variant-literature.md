# Search literature with an explicit variant term

## Question

Which publications match the stated AKT1 E17K literature query?

## 1. Retrieve and inspect

```sh
biomcp search article 'AKT1 E17K'
```

Read the echoed query, titles, source outcomes and problems. Search candidates require inspection for gene, variant and study context.

## 2. Retrieve and inspect

```sh
biomcp get article 33123537
```

Read the exact PMID, publication type and labeled abstract parts. Confirm the article’s stated variant and distinguish a review from the evidence it cites.

## Next

Follow references or full-text commands returned by the article. Inspect the original study before using its claims; a textual match does not establish that evidence applies to a patient.

Read response status, problems and source terms at every step. Live replies can change.
