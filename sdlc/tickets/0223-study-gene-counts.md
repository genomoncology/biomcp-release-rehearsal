## Outcome

This ticket starts only if 0207 proved a gene-filtered aggregate request. If 0207 found none, the ticket writes no code and brings the cost to the PM: the unfiltered BRCA reply is 4,258,105 bytes for one study. With the witness, `get study brca_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018 counts --gene TP53` resolves the gene through the existing checked gene resolution to Entrez 7157, sends the filtered request 0207 proved, and returns that gene's source aggregate row with exact Entrez ID and symbol, `numberOfAlteredCases`, `numberOfAlteredCasesOnPanel`, `totalCount`, `numberOfProfiledCases` and the native panel IDs, with a notice that the source states no unit. The `counts` section also returns the patient endpoint's `total-count` header with its endpoint and acquisition time, kept apart from the card's `allSampleCount`. The section adds the gene aggregate record, `--gene` and schema `biomcp.study_counts/1`, and the card gains its `counts` section step.

- Why: the 2026-10-26 study rulings accept the `counts` section and keep gene aggregates as source-specific metadata, and S21 to S24 and S26 ask what counts cBioPortal itself reports.
- Starts from: study.md's source-stated gene counts section and S21 to S26, the types table and the clinical review boundary, with the 2026-10-26 study rulings, which win over the design text; 0207's filtered-request witness and its MSK patient total; the j14214 aggregate and patient captures; the existing checked gene resolution; 0238's card and section dispatch; 0210's types.
- Rules: gene aggregates stay source-specific metadata. The shared `CohortObservation` subject stays `VariantRef`, and this ticket makes no shared-model change. No frequency, rate, prevalence or denominator is calculated, and `totalCount` never becomes events, `numberOfAlteredCases` never becomes patients and `numberOfProfiledCases` never becomes a derived denominator. The join uses exact native Entrez IDs only, with no symbol substring or disease-title match. A row whose Entrez ID differs from the requested gene is dropped with a problem and never cached, as closed issue 113 requires. A grouped row with several `entrezGeneIds` keeps its multiplicity and is never read as its first gene. A gene with no returned row reports a missing aggregate, never assayed and unaltered, and the study stays present. The patient total never fills a card field or a gene denominator. `counts` without `--gene` returns the patient total alone. `all` never sends a gene aggregate request. Source-wide `restricted` use stays, and a disabled source or group skips the section before cache or network.
- Proof: outside-in cases first, watched to fail. Before section code, one bounded keyless pm job captures each case's exact filtered request once, with zero retries, and records the replies. S21 and S22 assert BRCA's TP53 and KRAS rows; S23 and S24 assert MSK's TP53 and KRAS rows with both panel IDs; S26 asserts BRCA's patient total of 1084 apart from `allSampleCount`, and 0207's MSK total asserts a second patient positive; a gene with no returned row asserts a missing aggregate beside a present study. A synthetic cross-gene row asserts the drop, the problem and no cache admission, and a synthetic grouped row asserts kept multiplicity. Recorded 429, 500, timeout and broken-body cases assert a failed outcome with source and terms, never a zero. The job measures the section in CLI and MCP output, starting from the design's 281-byte projection for one gene row, and each case gets a ceiling at measured size plus 15 percent, recorded in study.md. Real replies replay from outside the repo through `--cases DIR`, and synthetic replies prove failure mechanics. No licensed content enters the repo. Then make landing-gate runs through pm job.
